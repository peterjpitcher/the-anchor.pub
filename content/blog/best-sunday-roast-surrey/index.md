## 1. The Anchor, Stanwell Moor

**Good for:** Families, dog owners, free parking near Heathrow

This one's ours. The Anchor is in the village of Stanwell Moor, seven minutes from Heathrow Terminal 5 and two minutes from M25 Junction 14. We're an independent village pub, not a chain, and Sunday is what we live for.

### The roast

Current options are all served with triple-cooked, herb-and-garlic crusted roast potatoes, seasonal veg and gravy:

- **Roast Turkey with Stuffing Ball**, carved fresh, with a sage and onion stuffing ball
- **Roast Pork Leg**, sliced to order with Bramley apple sauce
- **Roast Beef Topside**, 28-day topside, carved when you order it
- **Beetroot & Butternut Squash Wellington**, with our regular gravy, which is fully vegan
- **Kids Roast**, a smaller, child-sized portion of beef, pork or turkey

Yorkshire puddings come with the sliced roasts and the kids roast. The Wellington: Fully vegan as it comes. Ask if you'd like buttered cabbage or a Yorkshire pudding added, both of which make the plate no longer vegan. You'll find current prices on our [Sunday roast menu](/sunday-roast).

### What you need to know

Roasts are carved fresh every Sunday from 1pm to 6pm. There's nothing to order in advance, so walk in whenever suits you. Last seating is 5:30pm. Groups of 15 or more: a £10 per person deposit, fully deducted from your bill. The regular weekday menu, burgers, pizzas, fish and chips, is also available on Sunday.

We've 20 free spaces right outside. There's no time limit while you're with us, and nothing to register. Dogs are welcome throughout the pub, on a lead. We'll have water bowls and biscuits waiting. The beer garden seats 64, and at busy times a plane comes over about every 90 seconds (we're under Heathrow's flight path, and kids love it). We're outside the ULEZ zone.

[Book your Sunday roast at The Anchor](/sunday-roast) or call **01753 682707**.

## What Makes a Great Sunday Roast?

Every pub says it does a "proper" Sunday roast. Here's what to look for:

### Meat quality and cooking

The single biggest factor. Look for slow-roasted meat that's carved fresh for each plate, carved when you order it.

### Yorkshire puddings

They should be crisp on the outside and soft inside.

### Gravy

This is the tell. If the gravy is good, the kitchen probably cares about the rest too.

### Roast potatoes

Crispy outside, fluffy inside. If they're soft all the way through, something's gone wrong.

### Vegetables

Seasonal, with colour and bite. Not boiled into submission. Vegetables you actually want to eat, not just tolerate as the healthy bit of the plate.

## Tips for Getting a Great Sunday Roast in Surrey

1. **Book ahead where you can.** Popular pubs fill up, particularly in good weather.

2. **Arrive hungry.** A roast is a big plate. If you're having a starter, pace yourself.

3. **Ask about the meat.** Is it carved when you order it, or served from a carvery counter? Neither is wrong, but it's worth knowing what you're getting.

4. **Check the vegan and vegetarian options.** Look at the menu before you travel, so everyone at the table has something they'll want.

5. **Check the serving times.** Every pub's Sunday hours are different, so look before you set off.

6. **Combine it with something.** A walk before lunch makes the roast taste better. Box Hill, the North Downs, the Thames Path: Surrey has no shortage of options.
