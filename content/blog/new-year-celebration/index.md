The Anchor has been a **village pub near Heathrow** since 1751, on Horton Road in Stanwell Moor. It stood here before Heathrow existed, and today it's about seven minutes by car from Terminal 5.

## Regular nights

The dates vary, so [What's On](/whats-on) always has the next ones.

- **Quiz night:** once a month, currently on Wednesdays.
- **Cash bingo:** on set Wednesdays. See [cash bingo](/cash-bingo).
- **Music bingo:** hosted by Nikki Manfadge. See [music bingo](/music-bingo).
- **Curry Club:** a monthly rotating curry-night special.
- **Tasting nights:** every so often, expert-led and ticketed.

## Sunday roast

Roasts are carved fresh every Sunday from 1pm to 6pm. There's nothing to order in advance, so walk in whenever suits you. Last seating is 5:30pm.

## Live sport

We show live sport on BBC, ITV and Channel 4. We don't have Sky Sports or TNT Sports.

## Everyone's welcome

Dogs are welcome throughout the pub, on a lead. We'll have water bowls and biscuits waiting.

High chairs, buggy space and bottle warming on request are all here, and breastfeeding is welcome. We don't have baby changing facilities.

For a private party, see [private hire](/private-hire).

## Visiting

We've 20 free spaces right outside. There's no time limit while you're with us, and nothing to register.

Opening and kitchen times change by date, so check [find us](/find-us) before you come. You'll find us at Horton Road, Stanwell Moor, TW19 6AQ, or call 01753 682707. We're on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/theanchorpubsm/) and [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/theanchor.pub/).
