Our quiz runs once a month at The Anchor in Stanwell Moor, 7 minutes from Terminal 5. The owner hosts it, and it gets far too competitive.

## Monthly Quiz Night at The Anchor

### The Details

Here's how the night works:

**Quiz Night Details:**
- **Once a month**: arrive from 6:30pm for a 7pm start
- **Entry is £3 a person**
- **Hosted by the owner**, Peter Pitcher
- **Five rounds**: four of ten questions, plus an interactive quick-fire round in the middle, played on your phone
- **The prize is a £25 bar voucher** for the winning team, and a bottle of house wine for second from last
- **Maximum 6 people** per team

Dates are on [What's On](/whats-on).
