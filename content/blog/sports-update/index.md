## What sport we show at The Anchor

> We show live sport on BBC, ITV and Channel 4. We don't have Sky Sports or TNT Sports.

It's been terrestrial channels only since January 2025.

## How it works

We show the sport on our 4 TVs.

> The commentary's on for big games and tournaments.

Not sure whether a game's on one of our channels? Call us on 01753 682707 and we'll tell you. There's more on our [live sport page](/live-sport), and the rugby has its own [Six Nations page](/live-sport/six-nations).

## Coming to watch

- **Where:** Horton Road, Stanwell Moor, Surrey, TW19 6AQ, seven minutes from Heathrow Terminal 5.
- **Parking:** We've 20 free spaces right outside. There's no time limit while you're with us, and nothing to register.
- **Food:** served during kitchen hours. The times are on our [find us page](/find-us), and the dishes are on the [food menu](/food-menu).
- **Dogs:** Dogs are welcome throughout the pub, on a lead. We'll have water bowls and biscuits waiting.
- **A table:** [book one](/book-table) if you're coming as a group for a big game.
