Our Spring Tasting Night on 14 March 2025 was a sold-out evening of spirits, cocktails and good company in Stanwell Moor. Here's how it went.

## Spring Tasting Night: What Made It Special

### A Sold-Out Evening

Our March **spring spirits tasting** showed off seasonal flavours in our dining room. The night had:

- Warm, knowledgeable hosts
- A guided tasting of six spirits
- Cocktail demonstrations
- A seasonal quiz
- A small, friendly room

![spring tasting night at the anchor unmissable flav image](/content/blog/spring-tasting-night/image-1.jpeg)

### What We Tasted

We tried six spirits on the night: two flavoured vodkas and four gins. You'll find what we pour today on our [drinks menu](/drinks).

## How the Night Ran

![spring tasting night at the anchor unmissable flav image](/content/blog/spring-tasting-night/image-10.jpeg)

**6:30 PM - Welcome and Introduction**
- An overview of the evening's spirits
- Tasting notes and scorecards for everyone

**7:00 PM - Guided Tasting**
- A presentation of each spirit
- How each one is made
- Tasting techniques
- Food pairings with each spirit

**8:00 PM - Cocktail Session**
- Everyone made a seasonal cocktail
- A vote for the favourite spirit of the night

**9:00 PM - Quiz and Prizes**
- A spirit-themed quiz
- Prizes for the winners

## About Our Tasting Nights

Tasting nights at The Anchor are occasional, so there's no fixed date for the next one. They're ticketed, and for over 18s only. When one is planned you'll find it on our [what's on page](/whats-on), with the price and how to book.

**Call**: 01753 682707
**Visit**: The Anchor, Horton Road, Stanwell Moor, TW19 6AQ

### More Than Tastings

We also run **quiz nights**, monthly and usually on a Wednesday. See [what's on](/whats-on) for dates.

## Thank You for Coming

Thanks to everyone who joined us for the Spring Tasting Night. It was a lovely evening, and we hope to see you at the next one.
