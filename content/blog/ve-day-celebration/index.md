This is a historical update from The Anchor in Stanwell Moor about our VE Day week in May 2025. It was written ahead of the week, and everything in it has now finished. For what's on now, see [What's On](/whats-on).

For current food service, event dates and booking information, use the live pages on this website or call the pub.

## Why VE Day Matters

VE Day, or **Victory in Europe Day**, commemorates 8 May 1945, the moment when peace returned to Europe at the end of the Second World War. It was a day of church bells, street parties, and hopeful toasts. Families gathered around wireless radios. Communities danced in the streets. And in every town and village, local pubs threw open their doors to welcome the joyful chaos.

That spirit lives on at **The Anchor** in Stanwell Moor. In May 2025 we marked VE Day with a week of our own.

## What We Planned for VE Day Week 2025

### Quiz Night With a Patriotic Twist

On Wednesday 7 May 2025 our pub quiz had a special **VE Day-themed round** that nodded to the music, events, and icons of wartime Britain.

### Sing-Along Karaoke

On Thursday 8 May 2025 we held a karaoke night with a twist. Some songs were solo, and others were _group sing-alongs_ for the whole pub, from _We'll Meet Again_ to _Hey Jude_.

### Food and Drink

A food and drink offer ran from Tuesday 6 May to Sunday 11 May 2025. It has ended. For current dishes and prices, see the [food menu](/food-menu) and the [drinks menu](/drinks).

VE Day reminds us not just of where we've come from, but of what we share. For the nights we have coming up, see [What's On](/whats-on).
