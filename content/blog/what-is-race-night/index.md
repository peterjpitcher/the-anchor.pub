Ever been asked along to a **race night** and wondered what you've let yourself in for? It's a pub fundraiser built around recorded horse races. Here's how one works, and how ours ran when we held them at The Anchor in Stanwell Moor.

## What is a Race Night?

### Pub Entertainment with a Difference

A race night isn't another quiz or karaoke evening. It works like this:

- **Recorded races** - Real horse races, filmed earlier, are played on screen one at a time
- **Small bets** - You back a horse before each race
- **Prizes** - Winners collect after each race
- **Charity fundraising** - The night raises money for a good cause
- **Group entertainment** - It suits friends, families and work teams

Nobody in the room knows which race is coming or who wins it, so you don't need to know a thing about horses.

## How Our Race Nights Ran

We've held charity race nights at The Anchor in the past. This is how they went.

#### In the weeks before
- Betting cards went on sale at the bar
- You chose your horses from the race card
- Half the fun was talking tactics with your friends

#### On the night
- The races were shown on our TVs
- You could place more bets between races
- Prizes were handed out after each race
- The money raised went to charity

## Race Night Tips for First-Timers

**Betting Strategy:**
- Spread your bets across several races
- Back a horse because you like its name, it works as well as anything
- Pool your bets with friends
- Remember it's for charity
