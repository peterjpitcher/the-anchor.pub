These terms apply to the Quiz Night Guessing Competition taking place on the next scheduled Quiz Night event.

## 1. Promoter

The promoter is The Anchor, Horton Road, Stanwell Moor Village, Surrey, TW19 6AQ.

## 2. Who can enter

- The competition is open to people aged 18 or over.
- Employees of The Anchor, anyone involved in organising the competition, and anyone who has been given access to the correct answer before the competition closes may not enter.
- We may ask for valid photographic identification. Anyone unable to prove that they are aged 18 or over when requested will be disqualified.
- No purchase is necessary.

## 3. How to enter

- Entrants must submit one whole-number answer in response to the competition post in the designated WhatsApp group.
- Entries sent privately, posted elsewhere or received after the closing time will not be accepted.
- Only one entry is allowed per person. Where somebody submits more than one answer, only their first valid answer will be accepted and any later answers will be ignored.
- Entries that are edited after being posted may be disregarded. Entries deleted before the winner is selected cannot be verified and will not count.

## 4. Closing time

- Entries must be posted before 7:00pm on the day of the Quiz Night.
- The timestamp shown within WhatsApp will be used to determine whether an entry was received in time.

## 5. How the winner is selected

- The correct answer will be based on The Anchor’s records and will be revealed during the interval of Quiz Night.
- The valid entrant whose answer is numerically closest to the correct answer, and who is physically present at The Anchor when the winner is announced, will win.
- An entrant does not need to be taking part in the quiz, but they must be present on the premises as a customer at the time of the announcement.
- If the closest entrant is not present, their entry will be passed over and the prize will be offered to the next closest valid entrant who is present.
- If two or more eligible entrants are equally close, the entrant who submitted their valid answer first will win.
- If no valid entrant is present when the winner is announced, no prize will be awarded.
- The promoter’s reasonable decision regarding the correct answer, validity of entries, attendance and selection of the winner will be final.

## 6. The prize

The winner may choose one of the following:

- One pint of any available draught product
- One regular glass of house wine
- One standard single measure of a spirit from an available optic, with one standard mixer
- One soft drink

The prize is subject to product availability, the premises licence, responsible service requirements and The Anchor’s usual service policies.

Challenge 25 applies. The winner may be required to provide acceptable photographic identification before alcohol is served.

Alcohol will not be served where staff reasonably believe that doing so would be unlawful or inappropriate, including where the winner cannot provide suitable identification or appears intoxicated. In these circumstances, the winner may choose a soft drink instead.

The prize cannot be exchanged for cash, transferred to another person, increased to a double measure or combined with another offer.

## 7. Claiming the prize

- The prize must be claimed and consumed at The Anchor on the same Quiz Night after the winner is announced and before the premises closes.
- It cannot be saved, transferred, collected on another date or added as credit to a customer account.
- If the winner leaves without redeeming the prize, it will be forfeited.

## 8. WhatsApp and personal information

- Entries will be visible to other members of the WhatsApp group in accordance with the group’s normal settings.
- We will use an entrant’s WhatsApp display name, submitted answer and any information needed to confirm their eligibility only for administering this competition.
- The winner may be identified by their first name or WhatsApp display name in the group and during the Quiz Night announcement. Telephone numbers will not be republished as part of the winner announcement.
- Entering the competition will not add an entrant to any additional marketing list.

## 9. Technical problems and changes

- The promoter is not responsible for entries that cannot be seen or verified because of connectivity problems, deleted messages, WhatsApp faults or circumstances outside its reasonable control.
- The competition may only be cancelled or changed where reasonably necessary because of circumstances outside the promoter’s control. Any material change will be announced in the WhatsApp group and will be handled as fairly as reasonably possible.

By entering the competition, entrants confirm that they have read and accepted these terms.
