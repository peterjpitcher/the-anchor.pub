# SEO Growth Guide — Non-Technical Actions

This document covers the organic search growth activities that don't require code changes — things the pub team can action directly. Updated March 2026.

---

## 1. Google Business Profile (GBP) — Highest Priority

GBP is the most important factor for local search rankings ("pub near me", map pack results). These actions are free and high-impact.

### Weekly Actions (15 minutes)
- **Post an update** — Upload a photo with a short caption. Examples:
  - Monday: "What's on this week" with events listed
  - Wednesday: Photo of today's specials
  - Friday: Weekend opening hours reminder
  - Seasonal: Christmas menu, Valentine's, Mother's Day
- GBP posts appear directly in Google search results for branded searches

### One-Time Setup (do this month)
- **Q&A section**: Add your own questions and answers. Google lets anyone ask questions — if you seed the answers yourself, you control the narrative. Suggested Q&As:
  - Q: "Do you have free parking?" A: "Yes — 20 free spaces, no time limit for pub guests."
  - Q: "Can I walk from Heathrow?" A: "Not easily due to road layouts. A taxi takes 7–15 mins depending on terminal."
  - Q: "Do you accept dogs?" A: "Yes — dogs are welcome in the bar and beer garden."
  - Q: "Is there a Sunday roast?" A: "Yes, every Sunday. Pre-order by Saturday 1pm. Booking required."
  - Q: "Do you show live sport?" A: "Yes — Sky Sports and BT Sport. Check What's On for fixtures."
- **Categories**: Ensure all relevant categories are set. Should include: Pub, British restaurant, Bar, Event venue
- **Photos**: Upload at least 20 photos across: exterior, interior, food, beer garden, events, Sunday roast. Fresh photos outperform stock.
- **Attributes**: Ensure these are set: Free parking, Outdoor seating, Dogs allowed, WiFi, Accepts credit cards

### Ongoing (respond to everything)
- **Respond to every review** within 48 hours. Even negative ones. Google measures response rate and uses it in ranking.
- Use keywords naturally in responses: "Thanks for visiting us near Heathrow" or "We're so glad you enjoyed the Sunday roast at The Anchor"

## 2. Review Generation — High Priority

More reviews = better local pack rankings. The Anchor needs a consistent strategy, not a one-off push.

### The Easiest Method: Ask Verbally
- Train staff to say: "If you enjoyed your visit, a Google review really helps us. Our Google page is the-anchor.pub/leave-review"
- Best timing: after paying, when guest is happy
- Target: ask every table that had a good experience

### Supporting Methods
- **QR code on receipts/bills**: Print the /leave-review shortlink as a QR code on every receipt
- **Table cards**: Small card in the condiment holder: "Enjoyed your visit? Leave us a Google review" with QR code
- **WiFi landing page**: If guests connect to WiFi, show a review prompt page
- **WhatsApp**: After group bookings, send a thank-you WhatsApp with the review link

### Targets
- Current state: check Google Maps for current review count
- Target: 200+ reviews at 4.5★+ to dominate the local pack
- Realistic rate: 5–10 reviews per week with active asking = 200 reviews in ~5–6 months

### Do Not
- Ask for reviews in exchange for discounts or free items (violates Google policy)
- Write fake reviews or ask friends to review without having visited
- Respond aggressively to negative reviews (reply professionally always)

## 5. Backlink Outreach

Links from relevant local websites improve domain authority and rankings. These take time but have lasting value.

### Easiest Wins
- **Local community sites**: Stanwell Moor village website, Spelthorne Borough Council community pages
- **Visit Surrey / Surrey tourism**: Request a listing at visitsurrey.com
- **Local press**: Send newsworthy stories (charity events, milestone anniversaries, unusual events) to Staines & Ashford News, Surrey Live, Get Surrey

### Medium-Term Targets
- **Heathrow-adjacent sites**: If heathrow.com or any official Heathrow partner sites have "nearby dining" sections, request inclusion
- **Business travel publications**: Business Traveller magazine (businesstraveller.com) sometimes features local recommendations
- **Hotel concierge relationships**: If hotel concierges are recommending The Anchor verbally, ask if they'd add a link to their "local recommendations" page

### How to Pitch
Keep it simple. Email: "Hi, I run The Anchor pub in Stanwell Moor — we're a popular stop for Heathrow travellers and locals alike. Would you consider adding us to your local recommendations page? Happy to reciprocate with a mention on our blog."

## 6. Social Media (Indirect SEO Benefit)

Social signals don't directly affect rankings but build brand awareness and drive people to search for you.

### Facebook
- Post weekly minimum
- Event posts for all upcoming events (quiz nights, karaoke, drag shows, etc.)
- Food photos perform best — post Sunday roast photos every Sunday
- Respond to all comments and messages

### Instagram
- Focus on food photography — Sunday roasts, beer garden shots, event photos
- Use local hashtags: #HeathrowEats #StanwellMoor #SurreyPubs #PubFood
- Tag location in every post

### Video (Low effort, high return)
- Short videos of food being served, events, the beer garden — these perform extremely well on Facebook and Instagram and cost nothing to produce
