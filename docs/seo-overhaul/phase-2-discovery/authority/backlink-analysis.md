**Authority gap:** Significant. The Ostrich Inn benefits from accommodation-platform backlinks (Booking.com DA 95, Expedia DA 93, KAYAK DA 90) that The Anchor can never match without rooms. Their TripAdvisor presence as a hotel listing generates 10x the review volume. However, The Anchor's higher rating (4.9 vs 4.0) is a quality signal advantage.

**Key insight:** The Ostrich Inn's authority advantage is structural (accommodation platforms). The Anchor cannot close this gap directly but can build authority through different channels (aviation, entertainment, local community).

| Domain (parent) DA | 55-65 (greeneking.co.uk) | 10-15 |

| TripAdvisor reviews | 1,960 | 29 |
| Yelp reviews | 71 | ~5-10 |
| CAMRA listing | Yes | Yes |
| Unique content capability | Very limited (template page) | Unlimited |

**Authority gap:** Appears enormous due to Greene King's parent domain DA, but misleading. The Three Magpies page is a subdirectory of greeneking.co.uk with no unique content, no blog, and no ability to create targeted landing pages. The page inherits Greene King's domain authority but has almost zero page-level authority. Google increasingly weights page-level signals over domain-level for local queries.

**Key insight:** The Three Magpies' 1,960 TripAdvisor reviews are a genuine advantage for review-driven queries. However, The Anchor's independent domain with 195 indexed pages gives it far more surface area for long-tail queries. This is a permanent structural advantage.
