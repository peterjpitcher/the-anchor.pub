## Job Board Dominance

**Verdict: Job boards are overwhelmingly dominant for generic hospitality job searches.**

The top 5 positions for virtually every generic job search term are held by:
1. **Indeed** — dominates most terms, massive listing volume
2. **Reed.co.uk** — strong #2 across all terms
3. **Caterer.com** — dominant for hospitality-specific searches
4. **Totaljobs** — strong for location-based searches
5. **JOB TODAY** — growing presence, especially for bar/restaurant roles
6. **Leisurejobs.com** — strong for "pub" and "bar" specific terms

**An independent pub careers page cannot compete with these boards on generic terms.** A single page listing 2-3 roles will never outrank Indeed's 28,000+ Heathrow results or Caterer's 5,500+ hospitality listings.

However, there are two bypass mechanisms:
1. **Google for Jobs rich results** — JobPosting schema creates a separate widget above organic results
2. **Brand/location-specific searches** — where The Anchor already has domain authority

## Strategic Recommendations

### 1. Build the page, but set realistic expectations
The page will NOT rank for competitive terms like "bar staff jobs Staines" or "hospitality jobs near Heathrow." Those SERPs belong to Indeed, Reed, and Caterer. The page WILL rank for hyperlocal Stanwell Moor terms and can appear in Google for Jobs for broader terms via schema.

### 2. JobPosting schema is the single most important technical implementation
- Implement JSON-LD JobPosting markup for every open position
- Include: title, description, datePosted, validThrough, salary (even a range), employmentType, jobLocation with full address
- Use Google's Indexing API to push new/updated listings for fast indexing
- This is how you bypass job board dominance — you appear in the Google for Jobs widget alongside Indeed results

### 3. Also post on job boards and link back
- Post the same roles on Indeed (free tier), JOB TODAY (free for hospitality), and Caterer.com
- In each listing, link back to the-anchor.pub/join-our-team for full details
- This creates backlinks and drives candidates who discover you on boards back to your site
- It also means you appear in BOTH organic job board results AND Google for Jobs

### 4. Lean into what makes you unique vs. hotel/chain jobs
- Heathrow hotel bars offer shift work, corporate environments, conference service
- The Anchor offers: village pub atmosphere, free parking, no airport commute hassle, tips, Sunday roasts, regular customers, Greene King backing with independent feel
- These differentiators won't help with rankings but will convert visitors to applicants

### 5. "Near Heathrow" positioning is the key differentiator
- The proximity to Heathrow is the strongest angle for reaching job seekers
- Many Heathrow-area workers seek non-airport hospitality work with easier commutes
- "7 minutes from Terminal 5" is a genuine selling point for recruitment
- Target airport workers looking for a change of pace

### 6. Page should double as an employer brand page
- Even if organic traffic is modest, the page serves as a destination for:
  - Walk-in enquiry follow-ups ("check our website for roles")
  - Job board click-throughs
  - Social media recruitment posts (link to page)
  - Google Business Profile "Jobs" section
- The Duke of Wellington (Peach Pubs) is the closest template: culture, benefits, specific roles, clear apply mechanism

### 7. Update Google Business Profile
- Add job listings to the Google Business Profile if the feature is available
- This surfaces roles in Google Maps searches and local pack results
- Someone searching "pubs near me" in Staines could see job availability

### 8. Volume expectation: modest but sufficient
- Realistic organic traffic to the page: 10-30 visits/month from search
- Google for Jobs impressions: potentially 50-100/month with proper schema
- Job board referrals: depends on posting activity
- For a pub needing 1-3 hires at any time, this is sufficient reach
- The page's primary value is as a professional destination, not a traffic driver
