## SERP Landscape

### What currently ranks for key terms

**"bar staff jobs near Heathrow"** -- Top results are exclusively job aggregators:
1. Indeed UK (dominant across all queries)
2. Caterer.com (hospitality-specific, strong authority)
3. Reed.co.uk (general recruitment)
4. TotalJobs (general recruitment)
5. Leisurejobs.com (leisure/hospitality niche)
6. Jooble (aggregator)
7. JobToday (part-time/casual focus)
8. Heathrow Airport careers page (employer direct)

**"kitchen staff jobs Staines"** -- Same job board dominance. Fuller's and Premium Country Pubs appear as named employers within aggregator listings. The Swan Hotel in Staines has positions listed on Indeed.

**"pub jobs Stanwell Moor"** -- Very few results. The Anchor's own website appears (homepage), but no careers page. Leisurejobs shows generic Surrey bar/pub listings.

**"chef jobs pub near Heathrow"** -- Caterer.com and Indeed dominate. Named employers include Gordon Ramsay Plane Food (T5), BA Lounges, and chain pubs (Fuller's, Greene King managed houses). No independent pub careers pages rank.

### Key observations

- **No independent pub in this area has its own careers page ranking.** The SERP is 100% job boards and chain employer sites.
- **Greene King has a strong careers site** (jobs.greeneking.co.uk) but lists managed-house roles, not tenant pubs. The Anchor would not appear there.
- **Hyper-local terms** ("Stanwell Moor", "Spelthorne", "TW19") have very low competition -- an optimised page could rank.
- **Job boards use location + role title format** consistently (e.g., "Bar Staff Jobs in Surrey"). This is the content format searchers expect.
- **Google for Jobs** surfaces JobPosting schema results in a rich panel above organic results -- this is the primary ranking opportunity.

### Content format of top results

- Job title as H1
- Location prominently displayed
- Salary/pay rate where available
- Clear "Apply" CTA above the fold
- Bullet-point lists for requirements and benefits
- Structured data (JobPosting schema) triggers Google for Jobs panel

### Independent pub careers pages

- **Wadworth** (independent brewer, 19 managed pubs) has a "Work with us" page at wadworth.co.uk/work-with-us -- lists role categories, not individual postings
- **True Pub Co.** (19 sites, London) has a careers page on truepubco.co.uk/careers/
- **Urban Pubs & Bars** has workwithus page listing role types
- **Stonegate** (large chain) has a dedicated careers subdomain

No single-site independent pub was found ranking its own careers page for any of the target keywords. This means low organic competition but also low precedent -- the page must follow job board formatting conventions to meet searcher expectations.

## Content Brief

### Recommended URL

**`/join-our-team`**

Rationale: "/careers" and "/jobs" are more corporate. "/join-our-team" suits an independent village pub's tone, differentiates from job boards, and is used successfully by several pub groups. It also avoids confusion with the "/book-event" and "/book-table" paths already on the site.

### Page Title (meta title)

**Primary recommendation:**
`Bar & Kitchen Staff Jobs at The Anchor | Pub Near Heathrow, Stanwell Moor`

**Alternative:**
`Join Our Team | Bar & Kitchen Jobs | The Anchor Stanwell Moor`

Character count: 67 / 65 respectively (within 70-char limit).

`Experienced bar staff and kitchen team wanted at The Anchor, Stanwell Moor. Independent village pub 7 mins from Heathrow T5. Free parking, beer garden, great team. Apply now.`

### Schema maintenance rules

- **Set `validThrough`** to 3 months from posting (Google penalises stale listings)
- **Remove schema** when a role is filled (or set `jobStatus` to `Closed`)
- **Update `datePosted`** if the listing is refreshed/renewed
- **Do NOT include salary** unless the pub wants to publish pay rates (can add `baseSalary` later)
- **Validate** with Google Rich Results Test after deployment

### Additional schema on the page

The page should also carry the site-wide `LocalBusiness` schema that already exists across the site. No additional schema types needed beyond the two `JobPosting` blocks.

## Competitive Advantages for The Anchor

1. **Zero competition** -- No independent pub in the Stanwell Moor / Spelthorne area has a careers page. First-mover advantage for hyper-local terms.
2. **Google for Jobs** -- JobPosting schema will surface the listings in Google's job panel, bypassing the need to compete with Indeed/Reed in organic results.
3. **Heathrow proximity** -- The airport employment corridor creates a large pool of hospitality workers already commuting to the area. Terms like "bar jobs near Heathrow" capture workers who might prefer a pub over airport catering.
4. **Unique employer brand** -- Village pub atmosphere, beer garden under the flight path, independent feel -- none of the chain pubs or airport employers can offer this.
5. **Existing site authority** -- the-anchor.pub already ranks for local terms. A well-optimised /join-our-team page inherits that domain authority.
