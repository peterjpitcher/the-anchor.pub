### 2. Parking: the head terms are the biggest prize on the site and are not winnable

| Term | Volume | Competition | Bids |
|---|---:|---|---|
| heathrow terminal 5 parking | 50,000 | High, idx 72 | £0.94 to £2.64 |
| heathrow terminal 2 parking | 50,000 | High, idx 69 | £0.91 to £2.73 |
| heathrow terminal 3 parking | 50,000 | High, idx 70 | £0.85 to £2.56 |
| heathrow terminal 4 parking | 50,000 | Medium, idx 66 | £0.60 to £2.11 |
| heathrow parking | 50,000 | High, idx 75 | £0.78 to £2.33 |

200,000 searches a month across the four terminal terms, on a product the pub actually sells. Every one sits at competition index 66 to 75 with £2+ top-of-page bids, which is a SERP owned by Heathrow's own site and the parking aggregators. A village pub does not enter that top ten by writing a better page.

**Round 2 tested and killed the off-airport hypothesis.** Every "alternative parking" angle returned no data: `pub parking heathrow`, `non airport parking heathrow`, `heathrow parking stanwell`, `airport parking stanwell moor`, `parking near heathrow with shuttle`, `heathrow parking shuttle`, `heathrow parking alternatives`. People do not search for an alternative to airport parking. They search for the head term, or they search for the price.

**One term in 36 is both high-volume and winnable:**

> **`heathrow parking prices`: 5,000/mo, Low competition (index 22), bids £0.22 to £0.58**

Compare that against every other parking term carrying volume, which sits at index 51 to 86. It is informational intent ("what does it cost?"), which is precisely the query the aggregators answer badly because they serve a booking funnel instead of an answer. That is the acquisition play.

Also worth noting before investing: `heathrow parking` and `heathrow long stay parking` both show **YoY minus 90%**, as does `cheap long stay parking heathrow`.

### 3. On the landmark venue pages, Keyword Planner is blind and Search Console is right

Every landmark-specific term returned no data: `venue near staines registration office`, `venue near windsor register office`, `wake venue staines`, `funeral reception venue near me`, `function room for a wake`, `pub for funeral reception`, `funeral tea venue`, `baby shower venue near me`.

But the July plan recorded `/private-hire/near/slough-crematorium` at **28 clicks / 775 impressions / position 16.4** in Search Console.

GKP does not report below roughly 10 searches a month. These pages win a spread of micro-queries that individually never reach that floor but collectively convert. **Do not judge this page family on GKP data.** Keep them, differentiate the 77% duplicate pairs, and measure them in GSC only.

`wake venue near me` at 500/mo, Low (17), with **£1.69 to £5.06** top-of-page bids confirms the commercial value of the cluster. Those are the highest bids in the entire dataset.

## Avoid / negative keywords

- **halloween party ideas** (5,000, competition index **100**): craft and DIY intent, maximum advertiser competition. Wrong audience entirely.
- **heathrow terminal 2/3/4/5 parking** (50,000 each): real demand, but index 66 to 72 against Heathrow's own site and the aggregators. Do not build the page strategy on these, and do not bid on them.
- **pub parking heathrow, non airport parking heathrow, heathrow parking alternatives, airport parking stanwell moor**: no data. There is no "alternative parking" search behaviour to capture.
- **themed quiz night near me, tv themed quiz night, tv quiz night near me, soap quiz night, music quiz night near me**: no data. Demand attaches to named themes only.
- **pub near heathrow hotels** and the eight other zero-data hotel terms: do not rebuild a page around any of them.
- **secure parking near heathrow** (50, index 86): tiny volume, near-maximum competition.
- **Branded terms** ("the anchor stanwell moor"): already owned. Do not build pages against them.

## Per-cluster implementation guidance

### 1. `/halloween` (do this first, the window closes)

- **Title:** `Halloween Party Near Heathrow | Fancy-Dress Disco`
- **H1:** Halloween party near Heathrow
- **Intent:** someone deciding where to go on Halloween night, within about 20 minutes' drive.
- **Sections:** this year's date and theme · what the night actually is (fancy-dress disco) · prizes and judging · food before · free parking · getting here from Staines, Ashford, Feltham, Stanwell · book a table.
- **FAQ:** is it free to get in, do I have to dress up, is it family-friendly, what time does it start, do I need to book.
- **Cannibalisation:** `/whats-on` must link in, not compete. No separate "halloween events" page.

### 2. `/heathrow-parking` (retarget, do not rebuild four pages)

- **Title:** `Heathrow Parking Prices Compared | The Anchor`
- **H1:** What Heathrow parking actually costs
- **Intent:** working out the real price before booking anything.
- **Sections:** honest price comparison table (official car parks, aggregators, and The Anchor) · what each option includes · transfer time and how it works · the total cost including transfers · how to book.
- **The four terminal pages stay** but stop chasing their head terms. Rewrite each with genuinely terminal-specific detail (drive time, drop-off route, airlines served, early-flight timing) to fix the 64% duplication, and treat them as supporting pages linked from the hub.
- **Claims:** prices must come from live data. Never state a competitor's price without a dated source.

### 3. Themed quiz hub (new page)

- **URL:** `/quiz-night/themed`
- **Title:** `Themed Quiz Nights Near Heathrow | The Anchor`
- **H1:** Themed quiz nights at The Anchor
- **Intent:** a fan searching for a specific themed quiz, or a group looking for something different.
- **Sections:** what a themed quiz night is here · a section per theme run (Gavin and Stacey, Only Fools and Horses, and others as they run) · how the charity quizzes work · next dates · how to book.
- **Retire into it:** the dated past themed-event pages 301 here rather than to `/quiz-night`.
- **Cannibalisation:** `/quiz-night` keeps `pub quiz near me` (5,000, from the game-nights plan). The hub takes only the named-theme terms. Cross-link both ways.

### 4. `/restaurants-near-heathrow` (absorbs 12 pages)

- Add a **"staying at a Heathrow hotel?"** section covering the hotels by name, with real drive times. Serve the audience with a section, not eleven pages.
- 301 all 11 `/pub-near-*-heathrow` URLs plus `/heathrow-hotels-pub` here.
- Keep the July plan's head term; add `places to eat near heathrow airport` as a close variant.

### 5. `/food-menu` and `/sunday-roast` (retarget only, no rebuild)

- `/food-menu`: retitle and re-lead on **pub with food near me**. Current title targets menu terms with no data behind them.
- `/sunday-roast`: retitle and re-lead on **sunday roast near me**, keeping the Heathrow qualifier in the body copy rather than the title.
- Both are copy and metadata changes, not rebuilds.

| 11 hotel pages plus `/heathrow-hotels-pub` | total clicks and impressions, so the 301s can be judged honestly |
| `/private-hire/near/*` | all 17, since GKP cannot see them |

**Review windows:**
- `/halloween`: 4 weeks, then again the week after Halloween. Seasonal, so judge on peak not average.
- Everything else: 6 to 8 weeks post-deploy.
- Hotel 301s: 8 weeks, checking that `/restaurants-near-heathrow` absorbs the impressions rather than losing them.

**Which metric proves what:** impressions confirm visibility, CTR confirms the title and description are working, position confirms ranking. For the near-me terms, expect impressions to move well before position, because proximity ranking varies per searcher.

## Rationale

Two rounds and 90 keywords establish that this site's growth is not blocked by missing pages. It is blocked by pages pointed at terms with no demand behind them (11 hotel pages, zero measurable volume), pages pointed at terms they cannot win (four parking pages against 50,000/mo aggregator SERPs), and pages sitting on the smaller half of their own opportunity (`/sunday-roast` and `/food-menu` targeting Heathrow-qualified variants when near-me is 50,000 and 500,000).

Against that, three genuinely uncontested clusters are sitting unclaimed: Halloween at 10,000/mo and competition index 5, themed quizzes at 1,250/mo and competition index 0, and Heathrow parking prices at 5,000/mo and index 22. All three are winnable with pages that either already exist or take a day to write.
