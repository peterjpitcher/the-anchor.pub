# Keyword Plan: Game Night Pages (target country: UK)

**Pages:** `/quiz-night`, `/music-bingo`, `/cash-bingo`, `/karaoke`
**Audience:** local residents (Stanwell Moor, Staines, Ashford, Feltham, Bedfont, Egham, Colnbrook), not Heathrow travellers
**Data source:** Google Keyword Planner export, UK / English, Aug 2025 to Jul 2026, pulled 17 August 2026
**Rounds run:** 1 of up to 3 (see "What a Round 2 would add")

> GKP "Competition" and "Top of page bid" are **paid** signals. They are not organic difficulty.
> The account has no GSC link, so the Organic position and Organic impression share columns came
> back empty. There is therefore **no organic-difficulty evidence in this plan**, and every
> winnability call below is a judgement from the SERP landscape, not a measured figure.

### The important local finding: do not build town pages

**Twenty-four of the town-modified terms returned no data at all.** Specifically:

> bingo staines · bingo night staines · bingo night surrey · bingo ashford middlesex ·
> bingo near feltham · pub bingo surrey · quiz night staines · quiz night near staines ·
> quiz night stanwell moor · pub quiz ashford middlesex · pub quiz egham · pub quiz feltham ·
> karaoke ashford middlesex · karaoke egham · music bingo staines · music bingo feltham ·
> music quiz night near me · midweek quiz night · themed bingo night · pub games night near me ·
> pub with events near me · stanwell moor pub events · things to do in staines tonight ·
> whats on in staines this week

The local demand is not in town names. It is in **"near me"**, which Google resolves by the
searcher's proximity and your Google Business Profile. That means ranking for "karaoke near me"
reaches exactly the local audience you asked for, and building `/karaoke-staines`-style pages would
chase demand the data says is not there.

**Action: one honest location sentence per page, and put the effort into GBP instead.**

## Avoid / Negative Keywords

- **Unqualified bingo head terms** (`bingo staines`, `bingo near me` without "pub"): Buzz Bingo
  Feltham and the online bingo operators own this SERP, and GKP returned no data for the town
  variants anyway.
- **"karaoke bar"** as a *positioning claim*: target the query, never claim to be one. The Anchor is
  a pub that runs karaoke occasionally, and the SSOT forbids implying a fixed cadence.
- **"live music" anything**: discontinued in full per the SSOT. Not a keyword option at any volume.
- **name that tune bingo**: minus 100% on both trend columns.
- **Branded terms** (`the anchor stanwell moor`, `the anchor pub quiz`): already owned. Do not build
  new pages around these.

## Per-cluster implementation guidance

### `/karaoke`, the priority rebuild

- **Title tag:** `Karaoke Near Me | Free Entry Karaoke at The Anchor, Stanwell Moor`
- **H1:** `Karaoke Nights at The Anchor, Stanwell Moor`
- **Intent:** someone within a few miles wants to sing this weekend, and needs to know when the next
  one is, what it costs, and whether they will feel stupid.
- **Sections:** next confirmed date and time up front · free entry and "you do not have to sing" in
  the first screen · communal seating explained warmly · all-ages policy · how to get a song on ·
  short FAQ · booking.
- **Cannibalisation:** none. No other page targets karaoke.
- **Blocker:** no karaoke photography exists in the repo. This is the single largest remaining
  constraint on the page, given it is also the largest demand cluster on the site.

### `/quiz-night`

- **Title tag:** `Pub Quiz Near Me | Wednesday Quiz Night at The Anchor, Stanwell Moor`
- **H1:** `Wednesday Pub Quiz at The Anchor, Stanwell Moor`
- **Intent:** find a local quiz, check the price, check it is not intimidating, book a table.
- **Sections:** next date, 7pm to 9:30pm, £3, teams of six, all in the first screen · what the prize
  actually is · solo players matched on arrival · food until 9pm · FAQ.
- **Cannibalisation risk:** `/blog/quiz-nights-near-heathrow` is a round-up post that also ranks for
  quiz terms. Keep it informational and pointed at `/quiz-night`; do not let it target
  "pub quiz near me".

### `/music-bingo`

- **Title tag:** `Music Bingo Near Me | Drag Music Bingo at The Anchor, Stanwell Moor`
- **H1:** `Music Bingo at The Anchor, Stanwell Moor`
- **Intent:** find a themed, sociable night out that is not a quiz.
- **Sections:** next theme and date first · £5 · how it works in one line · Nikki as drag host (this
  is what earns the drag bingo cluster) · themes calendar · FAQ.
- **Cannibalisation:** `/cash-bingo` also uses the word bingo. Keep the two pages explicitly
  cross-linked and distinct: songs versus numbers.

### `/cash-bingo`

- **Title tag:** `Pub Bingo Near Me | Cash Bingo Nights at The Anchor, Stanwell Moor`
- **H1:** `Cash Bingo at The Anchor, Stanwell Moor`
- **Intent:** a traditional cash bingo night in a pub, not a bingo hall and not an app.
- **Sections:** £10 a book, cash only, in the first screen · what the £10 buys · snowball explained
  in two sentences · 18+ to play with supervised under-18s welcome · FAQ.
- **Cannibalisation:** as above with `/music-bingo`.

## Verdict on cutting page copy

The generic blocks flagged in the review ("Tips for a winning card", "Quiz team tips for the win",
"House rules", "Why everyone loves...", the Sunday-roast cross-sell cards) target **nothing in this
plan**. Removing them costs no ranking, because word count is not a ranking factor and none of that
copy matches a term with demand.

The net effect of implementing this plan is:

- `/quiz-night`, `/cash-bingo`, `/music-bingo`: **shorter and more relevant**
- `/karaoke`: **substantially longer and stronger**, because it is the biggest cluster and currently
  the thinnest page

## What a Round 2 would add

The stop rule is not fully met. A second GKP round would sharpen two things:

1. **Karaoke long-tail.** At 50,000/mo for the head term there is almost certainly a useful tail
   ("karaoke tonight near me", "pubs with karaoke near me", "karaoke this weekend", "karaoke night
   this friday") that Round 1 did not test.
2. **Drag bingo siblings.** "drag bingo", "drag queen bingo near me", "drag bingo london" would
   confirm whether the 500/mo is one term or a cluster.

Everything else has enough evidence to act on now.

## Measurement plan

- **Baseline now:** record GSC impressions, clicks, CTR and average position for the five head terms
  (`karaoke near me`, `pub quiz near me`, `quiz night near me`, `music bingo near me`,
  `bingo night near me`) and for the four URLs, before any of these changes deploy.
- **Review window:** 6 to 8 weeks after deploy.
- **Which metric proves what:** impressions means the pages became eligible; average position means
  the relevance work landed; CTR means the titles and descriptions are earning the click. Judge
  karaoke on impressions first, because it is starting from near-zero relevance on a very large term.
- **Link GSC to the Google Ads account** so future GKP pulls return the Organic position and Organic
  impression share columns. Their absence is the main gap in this plan.
