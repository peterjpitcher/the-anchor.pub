# SEO Copywriting Assessment — The Anchor, 7 July 2026

**Scope:** 15 priority pages (Strategy Lead §7 list), assessed from live crawl evidence (`evidence/page-metadata.csv`, 2026-07-07) and full page source in `app/`. SSOT compliance swept against `docs/SSOT.md` (read in full). No GSC/GA4/volume data this run; demand claims are inferred and marked as such. All rewriting routes to the `editorial-team` skill; this report specifies what to change and why.

## Summary

The June 2026 overhaul landed well at the body-copy level. The two biggest June open items I can verify as **done**: `/restaurants-near-heathrow` now has substantial, well-structured editorial body copy (2,359 words, terminal-by-terminal guidance), and the "9 draught beers" claim is gone (now "a good range of draught lagers and beers", `app/restaurants-near-heathrow/page.tsx:450`). `/sunday-roast` is the best page on the site: SSOT-clean, walk-in USP in the first 100 words, quotable answer blocks, live prices, correct vegan wellington wording.

The problems are concentrated in three places:

1. **Metadata is systemically broken, not editorially broken.** One title template (`app/layout.tsx:66`) doubles the brand and pushes 13 of 15 priority titles past 60 characters; one price formatter (`lib/menu-page-data.ts:172-190`) drops the £ symbol and breaks meta descriptions and visible price labels on two conversion pages. Two code fixes repair most of the metadata estate; page-by-page title rewrites are supplied below as the strings to feed that fix.
2. **Fact drift on the two priority-2 money pages.** `/private-hire/wakes` contradicts the SSOT (and itself) on capacity and crematorium distance; `/christmas-parties` hardcodes ~20 food/drink prices in violation of the live-pricing policy, right as the Jul–Oct booking window opens. These are the claims AI answers are already repeating (per `discovery/strategy/serp-snapshots.md`).
3. **Cannibalisation in the private-hire cluster.** `/private-hire` (H1 "Function Room Hire Near Heathrow & Staines") and `/function-room-hire` (H1 "Function Room Hire Near Heathrow") target the same term with near-identical H1s and titles. An interim retargeting is recommended below that does not pre-empt the SEO-009 data-driven keeper decision.

**Quality snapshot (content rubric, 1–5):** median priority page scores Relevance 4, Quality 4, Technical SEO 3 (metadata template drag), Competitiveness 3–4 (inferred, no rank data). Full per-page scores in `page-recommendations.md`.

## Pattern-level findings (fix once, not fifteen times)

### P1 — Title template doubles the brand and overruns 60 chars (Template/system fix)
`app/layout.tsx:66-67`: template `%s | The Anchor Stanwell Moor` is appended to page titles that already carry the brand, e.g. `/book-table` renders "Book a Table for Pub Food | The Anchor Stanwell Moor **| The Anchor Stanwell Moor**" (79 chars) and `/sunday-roast` renders 85 chars with "The Anchor Pub" *and* "The Anchor Stanwell Moor". Sitewide: 102/237 doubled, 211/237 over 60 chars (evidence/page-metadata.csv counts, per Strategy Lead). The root default (`app/layout.tsx:65`) also carries "The Anchor Pub" — SSOT §1 tolerates "The Anchor Pub" in title tags "where SEO value warrants it", but a fallback default is not a considered SEO choice, and the strategy decision this run is to strip it. **This confirms SEO-003; my contribution is the replacement title strings for all 15 priority pages (see page-recommendations.md), all ≤60 chars, brand exactly once.**

### P2 — `formatMenuPrice` drops the £ symbol (Template/system fix)
`lib/menu-page-data.ts:172-190`: `formatMenuPrice()` returns `String(numericPrice)` / `.toFixed(2)` with no currency symbol. Downstream damage on conversion pages:
- `/food-menu` meta description: "Dishes from 4." (`app/food-menu/page.tsx:118`)
- `/book-table` meta description: "Food from 4." (`app/book-table/page.tsx:39`)
- `/book-table` **visible** dish preview headings: "Chips(4)", "Fish Finger Wrap(10)" (crawl H3s; `formatPriceLabel`, `lib/menu-page-data.ts:187-190` renders `(${formatted})`)

Fix once in the formatter (emit `£4` / `£4.50`), then audit consumers so nothing double-prefixes ££. This half of SEO-006 is a one-line root cause, not a copy problem.

### P3 — Facts typed inline instead of drawn from SSOT-backed constants (Content process fix)
The wakes page *imports* `landmarks` (correct: "10 mins drive" to South West Middlesex Crematorium, `lib/local-seo-data.ts:20`) and then hardcodes "just five minutes" in prose on the same page (`app/private-hire/wakes/page.tsx:210` and `:410`). Same pattern with capacity: `lib/private-hire-capacity.ts` exists, yet the wakes page hardcodes "20 to 60 seated", "up to 50" and `maximumAttendeeCapacity: 50` — three values, none matching SSOT §8 (dining room 26 seated; private hire 10+ to 150). **Process rule for all future copy: numeric venue facts (capacities, distances, times) come from the SSOT-backed constants or are cross-checked against `docs/SSOT.md` at write time.** The editorial-team briefs should carry the SSOT extract for every fact used.

### P4 — Hardcoded food/drink prices surviving in two files (Content process fix + one-off fixes)
SSOT pricing policy: all food/drink prices live from the DB, never hardcoded. Violations found on priority pages:
- `app/christmas-parties/client-components.tsx:777-956` — ~20 hardcoded prices (trimmings boards £11.95/£21.95, pigs in blankets £3.95pp, wine bundle £39.00, beer bucket £27.00, tea & coffee £4.49pp, welcome drink £6.99pp, kids' squash £2.50, dessert bites £24/£45, cheeseboard +£3/£7.95, and a "£45-£52 spend target" line)
- `app/beer-garden/page.tsx:102` — hero lead "food from £10"

This matches the June handover note ("2 content files with hardcoded prices") — they are still live. Counter-example to copy from: the wakes and private-hire pages interpolate buffet prices live via `getLowestFoodPrice()` — "from £12pp"/"from £11pp" in those metas are **live values, not defects**.

## Top copy/fact corrections by commercial priority

1. **Wakes (priority-2 winner):** capacity story is incoherent (meta "up to 50" / schema 50 / body "20 to 60 seated" vs SSOT 26 seated in the dining room) and the crematorium distance is wrong in prose (5 min vs SSOT/lib 10 min). This is a grief-audience trust page that the strategy says is already winning; wrong logistics facts here have real-world cost (families planning around a 5-minute transfer that takes 10). Fix before any recrawl request.
2. **Christmas parties (in-window):** strip/replace hardcoded prices with live components before the Jul–Oct enquiry peak; fix "fifteen minutes from Terminal 2" (SSOT: 11 minutes); date or remove the £18–22 taxi-fare claim; remove the "£45-£52 spend target" phrasing (SSOT §11 bans publishing minimum-spend wording for private hire, and a "spend target" reads as exactly that).
3. **Private-hire meta description** (`app/private-hire/page.tsx:103`): 289 chars, grammatically broken ("family events for Private hire for 10+ to 150 guests"), and claims "no room-hire fee" which SSOT §11 only verifies for wakes ("Room hire charge: discuss on enquiry"). Rewrite supplied; owner to confirm the room-fee claim before it is reused anywhere.
4. **Whats-on meta** overclaims: "Pub events near Heathrow this week" / OG "Weekly pub events" + evergreen karaoke promotion. SSOT §10: quiz and cash bingo are monthly, karaoke is occasional and only promoted when listed. Rewrite supplied.
5. **Homepage title has no brand** (`app/page.tsx:32` — Next.js does not apply the layout template to the root segment): "Pub Food in Stanwell Moor | 7 Mins from Heathrow T5". Navigational searchers looking for "the anchor stanwell moor" get a SERP title with no entity name; weak for brand SERP and AI entity grounding. Recommended: "The Anchor, Stanwell Moor | Pub Food Near Heathrow T5" (54).
