# Content Quality Audit — Editor / QA Lead
**Run:** 2026-07-07 Full Overhaul · **Sources:** codebase @ main (19e88215), docs/SSOT.md + SSOT.json (canonical), evidence/ crawl (240 pages), strategy-document.md, inputs/input-summary.md. No GSC/GA4/CrUX this run — no demand or ranking claims are made in this report.

## Summary

The 15 priority pages are in much better factual shape than the long tail. The core money pages — /sunday-roast, /book-table, /beer-garden, /whats-on, /plane-spotting-heathrow, /near-heathrow (body copy), /food-menu (walk-in copy), /heathrow-parking — pass the SSOT accuracy check cleanly: walk-in Sunday roast rules are correctly stated everywhere (all 18 blog "pre-order" mentions are negations or SSOT-permitted private-event pre-orders), the wellington is consistently "vegan", deposits are stated with the SSOT standard copy, and the seasonal pages (halloween, fathers-day, mothers-day, new-years-eve) are exemplary — code comments actively forbid inventing offers, and none do.

The systemic problem is **the June price-scrub half-landed**. A search-and-replace pass swapped hardcoded prices for placeholder phrases, and those placeholders now render as literal customer-facing text: "Pigs in blankets, live price" on /christmas-parties (a priority page entering its Jul–Oct booking window), "buffets priced from the live approved source" in the /private-hire/engagement-parties meta description (visible in SERP snippets), and "there's usually pricing discussed on enquiry" mid-sentence in blogs. Meanwhile other files were never scrubbed at all and still carry full hardcoded price tables. A second systemic issue: the estate contradicts itself on room-hire fees ("no room-hire fee" vs "a room hire fee applies" vs "£0 room hire" — SSOT says "discuss on enquiry"), and the wakes page contradicts itself on capacity within one page.

June leftovers adjudicated: (1) hardcoded prices — worse than the "2 files" June flagged; confirmed in 2 app pages + at least 3 live blog posts; (2) the "/restaurants-near-heathrow 9 draught beers" claim is **fixed** (now "a good range of draught lagers and beers"), but the same defect class re-appeared on /our-pub ("seven draught lines… Pints start from £4.95"); (3) "The Anchor Pub" — **zero occurrences in body copy**; the 10 title-field uses are explicitly permitted by SSOT §1 ("page titles, alt text, schema name fields") — the strategy doc's "banned-phrase titles" framing is stricter than the SSOT and should be corrected (retitle under SEO-003 for dedup/length reasons, not as a compliance issue); (4) seasonal pages — no invented event details found; the A11 gating system works.

AI-pattern spot-checks came back clean (0 hits for the standard hollow-phrase tells across app/ and content/blog). The site's editorial voice is consistent and human.

## Critical Quality Issues

1. **Placeholder text renders in live customer copy** ("live price" ×9 on /christmas-parties; "buffets priced from the live approved source" in engagement-parties meta description, JSON-LD, hero and FAQs; "at live prices" on anniversary-parties; "current approved price-current approved price" in the pub-vs-hotel blog comparison table). Customers see internal jargon instead of prices on the exact pages meant to convert Christmas and private-hire enquiries. This is the single most trust-damaging defect found.
2. **Enquiry CTAs pointing at non-SSOT email addresses** (info@the-anchor.pub, events@the-anchor.pub) on live pages including two private-hire-adjacent pages and three Christmas blog posts. SSOT §2: manager@the-anchor.pub is the only correct email. If those mailboxes are dead, enquiries are being silently lost.
3. **Room-hire fee stated three contradictory ways across the private-hire cluster** — the priority-2 revenue path. SSOT says "Discuss on enquiry; do not publish minimum-spend wording"; pages variously claim "no room-hire fee" (/private-hire meta), "a room hire fee applies" (4 occasion pages), and "£0 room hire" (2 blogs).
4. **Explicit banned claims still live in the blog**: "Use traditional beef dripping" (fish-chips-guide — SSOT §14 verbatim ban), "Rotating guest ales" (friday-extended-hours), "Baby changing facilities" + "Kids eat free" (euro-2024-viewing).

## Brand Voice Findings

Voice is consistent: friendly, cheeky, first-person plural, British English, and notably honest about what the pub doesn't have (no Sky, no accessible toilet, kitchen closed Monday) — that honesty is a genuine trust asset and matches SSOT tone. Two deviations: (1) placeholder leaks (see Critical #1) read as broken robot-speak and are the biggest voice violation on the site; (2) em-dash usage was not audited exhaustively but the SSOT punctuation rule (no em dashes in customer copy) should be added to any content lint.

**"The Anchor Pub" adjudication (June leftover #3):** zero body-copy occurrences. The 10 title-field occurrences (app/layout.tsx:66 default title, join-our-team, function-room-hire, sunday-roast, private-party-venue + crawl-visible variants in evidence/page-metadata.csv) are expressly permitted by SSOT §1: *"Use 'The Anchor Pub' only where SEO value warrants it (page titles, alt text, schema name fields)."* The strategy document's classification of these as "banned-phrase titles" overstates the SSOT rule — SSOT is canonical. Retitling under SEO-003 remains sensible for brand-doubling/length reasons, but it is a style decision, not a compliance defect. The orchestrator should carry this correction into the roadmap wording.

## Content Quality Standards Recommendations

1. **Finish the price-scrub properly (process fix, not page fixes).** Define the three approved patterns — (a) interpolate live price, (b) price-free copy, (c) SSOT-confirmed fixed figure (deposits, ULEZ, quiz/bingo) — and sweep every remaining `£` in app/ and content/blog against them. The grep is one line; the current state (placeholders rendering + totals that reverse-engineer scrubbed prices) is worse than either finishing or not starting.
2. **Add banned-claim and placeholder lint to CI.** The drift-guard test exists for SSOT-vs-JSON; extend it (or add a sibling test) to grep built content for: banned phrases (§14 list), placeholder strings ("live price", "approved source", "pricing discussed on enquiry"), non-SSOT emails, and hardcoded £ outside the approved fixed list. Every finding in this report except the drive-time errors would have been caught mechanically.
3. **One approved sentence per volatile fact.** Room-hire fee, capacity, crematorium distances, and contact email each need a single SSOT-blessed sentence reused verbatim (the deposit copy already works this way and is flawless across the estate — copy that model).
4. **Historical-update banner policy for the blog.** stanwell-moor-brew and pravha-beer show the correct pattern; friday-extended-hours and euro-2024-viewing show what happens without it. Rule: any post asserting hours, offers, broadcasts, or facilities gets the banner or a 301 when reality changes.
