## The one-line strategic read
Visibility is largely **won** — The Anchor already ranks at/near the top for its commercial clusters and is the AI-cited entity for plane-spotting, wakes, pub-near-T5 and Sunday roast. The money is **not** in chasing new rankings; it is in (a) proving conversions are measured at all, (b) stopping ranked traffic from hitting broken prices, wrong facts, dead forms and mis-routed CTAs, and (c) tidying systemic template defects. This cycle is **fix-and-protect**, not build. No net-new pages recommended.
