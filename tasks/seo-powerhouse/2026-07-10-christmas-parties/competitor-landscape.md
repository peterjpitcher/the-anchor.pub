## Patterns worth adopting

- Separate party and festive-meal routes rather than forcing both through one generic enquiry label.
- Put dates/availability, format, inclusions, capacity and booking conditions near the CTA.
- Make the next action obvious: enquire for a party, or request a pre-order lunch/dinner.
- Use scannable package cards and essential facts; do not require users to read a long guide before enquiring.

## Differentiation to protect

- Traditional village-pub setting rather than hotel or chain-party framing.
- Heathrow proximity with free on-site parking and outside-ULEZ access.
- Equal support for workplace parties and sit-down family/team meals.
- Direct contact with the venue and a clear, explicit pre-order process.

The competitor set supports the two-journey recommendation. It does not validate search volume, ranking position or a claim that one competitor receives more organic traffic.
