# Content Strategy Report

Date: 2026-07-10  
Scope: `/christmas-parties` and its two supporting Christmas URLs

## Executive decision

Keep and optimise `/christmas-parties`. It is indexable, self-canonical, in the sitemap and already receives 30 sampled internal links. The immediate commercial problem is not a lack of content. It is that 4,569 words serve two different booking needs while the front of the page and form still divide visitors into “Dinner (up to 25)” or “Buffet (26+)”.

The page should immediately offer:

1. **Plan a Christmas party** for private, shared, buffet, entertainment or drinks-led needs.
2. **Request a sit-down Christmas lunch or dinner** with **pre-order only** visible beside the choice.

No GSC, GA4, keyword-volume, rank or backlink data was available. Search performance, demand and cannibalisation are therefore unavailable. Manual SERP evidence supports intent and page-format decisions only.

**Implementation update:** The main page now provides the two routes above, uses the shorter metadata and H1, shows pre-order-only meal wording, and has removed or qualified the unsupported operational and menu claims identified in the baseline review. The scores below describe the collected baseline, not the final edited source.

## Safest high-impact changes now

### 1. Make the two choices explicit above the fold

Recommended front-of-page copy:

- **Title source:** `Christmas Parties Near Heathrow | 2026`
- **H1:** `Christmas parties and festive dining near Heathrow`
- **Lead:** `Plan a Christmas party, or request a sit-down Christmas lunch or dinner by pre-order. We are seven minutes from Heathrow Terminal 5, with free parking and flexible space for groups.`
- **Party CTA:** `Plan a Christmas party`
- **Meal CTA:** `Request a festive lunch or dinner`
- **Meal helper:** `Sit-down Christmas meals are available by pre-order only.`
- **Meta description:** `Plan a Christmas party near Heathrow, or request a sit-down Christmas lunch or dinner. Festive meals are pre-order only. Free parking. Enquire for live pricing.`

This is a low-risk metadata, heading and microcopy change. Keep the previous strings in the change log for one-commit rollback.

### 2. Add one early, source-aligned booking-facts block

Use a short visible block before the long menu content:

| Fact | Approved wording |
|---|---|
| Party route | `Tell us your group size, preferred date and the type of party you want.` |
| Meal route | `Sit-down Christmas lunch and dinner are available by pre-order only.` |
| Christmas capacity | `Up to 60 seated or 200 standing, depending on layout and date.` |
| Buffet | `Festive buffets are available for 26 or more guests.` |
| Deposit | `Every Christmas menu booking requires a £10 per person non-refundable deposit.` |
| Pricing | `Ask for the current festive menu and live pricing when you enquire.` |
| Access | `Around 20 free parking spaces, seven minutes from Heathrow Terminal 5.` |

Do not repeat general private-hire capacity of 10 to 150 as the Christmas capacity. Do not publish a menu price unless it comes from the approved live source.

### 3. Rewrite keyword-first FAQs without deleting the whole section

Safe replacements:

- Replace `Can I find you by searching "xmas party near me"?` with `Where is The Anchor?`
- Replace the long “Christmas lunch near me” answer with: `Yes. Sit-down Christmas lunches and dinners are available by pre-order only. Tell us your preferred date, service, guest count and dietary needs when you enquire.`
- Replace unsupported exact availability pressure with: `Popular December dates can fill early. Enquire for current availability.`
- Keep the deposit answer only in the SSOT-approved form: £10 per person, all Christmas menu bookings, non-refundable.

This keeps the useful FAQ structure while removing the clearest made-for-search wording.

### 4. Soften or verify unsupported commercial claims

The page currently claims a seven-day pre-order deadline, a two-hour table time, optional 10% service charge, substitution surcharges, Friday and Saturday sell-outs by October, a one-working-day response, a no-spreadsheet pre-order system and three attributed testimonials. These claims were not found in the reviewed SSOT evidence.

For immediate release, either obtain a current approved source or use non-specific wording such as `We will confirm the pre-order deadline and final details with your booking.` Testimonials should remain only if the business can identify their source and permission record.

## Content architecture

- `/christmas-parties`: commercial hub, venue facts, two booking choices, menu/terms summary and enquiry.
- `/corporate-christmas-parties`: office-organiser support, internal approvals, VAT, logistics and group coordination.
- `/blog/cheap-christmas-parties-heathrow`: informational budgeting guide with no stale prices.
- Keep the current URLs and canonicals. No new Christmas URL is justified without demand data.
- Place support-page links after the main offer and booking facts, not before the primary conversion choices.

### Acceptance criteria

- Both booking choices are visible above the fold on desktop and mobile.
- `Pre-order only` appears beside every sit-down meal entry point.
- No Christmas capacity conflicts with 60 seated and 200 standing.
- No stale or unapproved price appears.
- FAQs answer real booking questions in natural language.
- Every operational claim has an approved source or uses qualified wording.
- The previous metadata and copy remain available for rollback.
