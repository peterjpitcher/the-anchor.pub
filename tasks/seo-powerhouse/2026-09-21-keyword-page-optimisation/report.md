## Overlap controls

- The plane-spotting guide owns comparison and viewing-area searches. The commercial page owns searches for a pub where people can watch aircraft.
- The restaurants page owns general Heathrow dining. The menu page owns menu and dish searches. The pre-flight page owns timing and pre-flight intent.
- The private-hire hub owns broad room and space searches. Occasion pages only own their specific event type.
- The Heathrow things-to-do guide owns visitor discovery. `/whats-on` remains for pub events and entertainment.

## Search Console and analytics findings

- The current sitemap is submitted, was read on 21 September 2026, has status `Success`, and contains 201 discovered pages.
- All 34 checked commercial targets return 200, have a self-referencing canonical, a distinct title and an H1.
- The recent traffic fall is concentrated in the two plane-spotting pages and beer-garden page. It is not a site-wide indexing failure.
- The restaurants page has strong impressions but sits just outside page one, so the priority is authority and monitoring rather than another rewrite.
- The food-menu page is already aligned to menu intent and uses live prices. Its low click-through rate should be monitored after the current title has accumulated enough data.

## Google Business Profile

The category, service, description and highlight edits have been submitted. Google still displays `Live music` publicly while the edit is under review. The website and profile should not be changed again until Google finishes that review.

## Limitations

Keyword Planner figures are broad bands, not exact search counts. Search Console evidence is whole-property rather than UK-only. The page-indexing export contains reason totals but not the example URL lists, so the 666 excluded URLs cannot be classified individually from this export alone.
