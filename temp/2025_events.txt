Cheers to Celebrations
Welcome to The Anchor Pub, where we celebrate life's special
moments with warmth, joy, and unparalleled attention to detail. We
understand that your personal celebrations are more than just events;
they're memories in the making, cherished moments shared with
loved ones that will be remembered for a lifetime. That's why we take
great pride in providing a warm and welcoming atmosphere where
you can relax, unwind, and focus on creating beautiful memories with
the people who matter most to you.
From the moment you step into our pub, you'll be greeted with a
sense of comfort and hospitality that's second to none. Our friendly
staff is always on hand to help you plan the perfect personal
celebration, whether you're looking for a cosy and intimate space or a
grand and festive setting. With a range of indoor and outdoor spaces,
private dining rooms, and garden areas, we have the perfect space to
suit your needs and preferences.
So, whether you're planning a milestone birthday, a christening, a
baby shower, or a wake, we invite you to celebrate with us at The
Anchor Pub. With excellent food and drink options, ample parking,
and a pet-friendly environment, we have everything you need to
make your personal celebration unforgettable. Let us be your hosts
and create beautiful memories together!

Space to Celebrate
At The Anchor Pub, we understand that the right event space can
make all the difference in creating a memorable and enjoyable
experience for you and your guests. That's why we offer a range of
versatile event spaces to suit any occasion, from cosy and intimate
gatherings to large and lively celebrations.
THE DINING ROOM
Looking for a cosy and intimate space to host your
personal celebration? Our dining room is the perfect
choice. With lots of natural light and beautiful french
doors, this private space offers views of the outdoors
while providing a warm and intimate ambiance. Seated
capacity for up to 26 guests or standing capacity for up to
50 guests.
THE GARDEN
On a sunny day, our Garden area is the perfect spot for
hosting a large group of guests. With plenty of seating
and ample space to mingle, your guests can enjoy the
great outdoors while celebrating your special occasion.
This space can accommodate up to 64 seated guests
and up to 150 standing guests.
THE MAIN AREA
Want to host a big party with a lively atmosphere? Look
no further than our Main Pub area. With a bar running
along one wall, this spacious area is perfect for hosting
disco's, DJ's, and live music. This space can
accommodate up to 50 guests seated inside, 64 guests
seated outside, and up to 300 standing guests.

Feast Your Eyes
Discover delicious catering options that will take your event to the next level. From
savory finger foods to exquisite three-course menus, our catering options are tailored
to fit any occasion, including birthday parties, christenings, wakes, and more. Our
menu features a variety of freshly prepared options, served by our attentive staff to
ensure a seamless and unforgettable experience for you and your guests. Whether
you're looking for a classic sandwich buffet, a hot finger buffet or a premium
charcuterie board, our food options are sure to satisfy your guests' cravings.
SANDWICH BUFFET
Our sandwich buffet features a variety of freshly prepared sandwiches, including classic options
such as cheese, ham, and vegetarian options like egg and watercress. This buffet is served with a
side of mixed greens and crisps and crudatés for a satisfying and delicious lunch experience.
FINGER BUFFET
Our hot finger buffet features a variety of delicious, savory options such as sandwiches, chicken
goujons, cocktail sausages, sausage rolls, mini pizzas, and spring rolls; all served warm and perfect
for finger foods.
PREMIUM BUFFET
Our premium buffet features charcuterie boards with cheese, roasted ham, topside of beef, and rolls
for people to make up as they prefer. We also lay out mixed leaf salad, potato salad, and coleslaw,
chicken goujons, cocktail sausages, mini quiches, sausage rolls, and spring rolls.

Our kid's party menu is perfect for any child's celebration, featuring delicious options

MINI PIZZA & CHIPS
Our freshly stone-baked pizzas come in two classic flavours - Rustic Classic or Simply Salami - and

Bring Your Own Food
We understand that sometimes customers may have specific dietary requirements
or preferences, or may simply want to bring their own food to a celebration. That's
why we offer a BYOF (Bring Your Own Food) option, allowing customers to bring
their own food to their event. Customers must sign a waiver prior to the event to
ensure that all food brought onto the premises is safe and suitable for consumption.
We are happy to work with customers to ensure that their event runs smoothly and
that their guests have a great time, regardless of whether they choose our catering
options or to bring their own food.
